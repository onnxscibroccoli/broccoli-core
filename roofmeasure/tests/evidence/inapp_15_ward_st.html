Accuracy and limitations

  • Eave/rake classification is a heuristic from the 2-D roofprint outline, not a 3-D roof model; verify each edge on imagery or on site.
  • Pitch is LiDAR-derived (lidar:usgs_3dep_ept:MA_CentralEastern_1_2021); confidence 0.95. Mixed pitches are reduced to one dominant value.
  • Ridge/hip/valley lengths come from a straight-skeleton model of the 2-D roofprint at a single pitch (facets lane), not a measured 3-D roof; dormers and mixed pitches are not modelled.
  • Roofprint outlines may be offset or dated relative to current construction (additions, dormers, porches).
  • For estimating only — NOT for engineering, permitting, structural or insurance purposes. Verify all measurements on site before ordering.
