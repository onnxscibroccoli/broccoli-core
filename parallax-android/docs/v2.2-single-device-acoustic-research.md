# Parallax Distortion 2.2 — Single-device sensing and audio research

Status: **experimental Android implementation**. Build/unit test results are recorded by GitHub Actions; physical acoustic accuracy, wearable/headphone safety, in-the-room detection, and microphone transfer function are not verified. A successful unit test cannot certify presence detection, accurate mapping or health benefits. Multi-device cooperative sonar is **optional future work**, not a prerequisite and not a test dependency.

## Implemented v2.2

- Package `com.parallax.entrainment.v22`, distinct from v2.0/v2.1.
- Home-screen controls mirror bell enabling, selectable fundamental, singing bowl timbre, binaural bell carrier, hourly chime and Auto EQ entry.
- Stereo bell carrier: separate bell voices; right fundamental equals left plus selected beat rate. Requires stereo headphones for an interaural binaural perception; phone speaker outputs are not perceptual binaural stimulation.
- 16 bell fundamentals, separate modal bowl sound with split mode pairs, adjustable 2–60s ring and 4–300s repetition. A long ring may constrain the minimum repeat interval.
- Hourly chimes only during an existing **active audible session**; no exact-background-chime promise, device wakeup, or covert service.
- Auto EQ adaptive fallback: four reliable spectral bands are required; unreliable bands preserve their previous gains. Still rejects clipping and calls; does not infer perfect acoustic flattening.
- Single-device **explicitly started** 48kHz/20ms 17.5–21.5kHz chirp experiment; matched-filter relative direct-path and candidate echo estimate. Short PCM buffers are discarded. Controls indicate real measurements and uncertainty.
- Android game-rotation-vector and accelerometer checks for relative yaw and motion flags. **No full inertial navigation or ES-EKF**; a single echo is not a mapped geometric landmark.

## Known limitations and physics

1. `c/(2B)` = **4.29cm ideal waveform range resolution** at B=4000Hz; *not* realized error bound. Phone mic/speaker rolloff, clock drift, direct leakage, latency, multipath, and side lobes dominate.
2. At 48kHz Nyquist is 24kHz; microphone quality above ~17kHz is **not guaranteed** by Android's nominal 48kHz support or the UNPROCESSED source designation.
3. A speaker plus one microphone measures only radial delay candidates after a direct-path alignment, not azimuth, person identity, geometry, or absolute device position.
4. The v2.2 experiment cannot distinguish an ordinary wall return from a person; do not label output as verified human/presence detection.
5. Source `UNPROCESSED` may fall back to vendor DSP. Audio cancellation effects and stereo routing may limit use. Detect each path and fail without fabricating data.
6. A 20ms waveform can be repeated at 50Hz **in theory**; this app's first hardware probe is deliberately one-shot. Hardware performance cannot be asserted without on-device end-to-end tests.
7. Low-volume near-ultrasound may still be audible to children or animals. Do not run around sleeping people or pets. Sonar starts **only after visible per-measurement consent**.
8. Auto-EQ mic response and room + speaker response are convolved; no arbitrary inverse FIR can guarantee ±1dB flattening without a calibrated reference. Never boost deep spectral nulls.
9. The proposed 15-state error EKF has no complete observable range-to-known-boundary measurement model at this stage. Integrating acceleration to position alone would yield misleading drift. Introduce EKF only after reliable anchor association, clock calibration, and synthetic+physical error statistics.
10. Ultrasonic metasurfaces require engineered physical hardware. Software can test conventional directional filtering when independently addressable microphones are actually exposed; cannot synthesize a physical metasurface from a mono transducer.
11. PINNs may be useful offline for waveform simulations, but no trained model, dataset, or validated hardware-domain generalization is included. Keep entirely optional.
12. The nine original 174–963Hz presets and added 110/136.1/220/256/330/432/440 Hz are *listening* and tuning options, not proven neurological therapy.

## Required single-device testing (Samsung A14 Android 15)

1. Confirm audio starts in **Play Alongside**, never silently resets to OFF without explicit action. Enable bells on home screen, start a session, and verify sound.
2. With headphones, alternate a plain binaural tone and bell-carrier voice: determine whether left/right voices differ at the selected beat; verify toggles never affect another app's volume.
3. Set sustain 30s / repeat 50s, toggle bowl mode; measure click-free transitions and safe output at low volume. Test hourly chime only while audible session exists.
4. Test Auto EQ in quiet/noisy room, with weak mic, volume changed, and Bluetooth route. Record non-private **diagnostic counts** of valid bands and clip %, verify previous correction retained on failure.
5. Explicitly grant mic, run one sonar test in an empty quiet room, then place a flat reflector at approximately 0.5m/1m. Record if a stable candidate echo emerges; also test no target, phone moving, low volume and mic permission denied.
6. Disconnect Bluetooth and disable mic; ensure no background capture. See Android's microphone privacy indicator.
7. Validate TalkBack, large fonts, permission interruption, device rotation, full vs border overlay, and battery.
8. A/B compare a calibrated measuring microphone **if one becomes available**; this is not required for the initial exploratory single-device test.
9. No second phone, remote workstation, root, device flash, Wi-Fi, or cloud data needed.

## Engineering roadmap / gate criteria

- **Gate A:** CI unit, lint, assemble pass and APK installs. No crash on denied permissions.
- **Gate B:** Single-device capability probe measures reproducible direct leakage and candidate echo with confidence; if not, mark “hardware unsupported” and offer safe passive sound/motion diagnostics.
- **Gate C:** Repeat a stationary reflector test at measured distances; characterize absolute/relative error (median, 95th percentile), false positives, audible byproducts and thermal/battery cost.
- **Gate D:** Only then implement reference/echo time synchronization, robust deconvolution/CFAR clutter suppression, motion tracks, IMU timestamp alignment, measurement association and bounded 15-state ES-EKF; evaluate observability.
- **Gate E:** Oboe C++ optimized path, optional device-dependent 2-mic beamforming and offline physics-informed methods after Kotlin reference accuracy is measured. No claim of <8ms latency from a requested API flag.
- **Optional future:** Multi-device cooperative sonar; not required to test the product.
