# The Inversion

Right now, the stack looks like this:

```
[You] → phone → carrier → OEM → ad network → government
         ↑         ↑        ↑         ↑            ↑
    voice      touch    gaze    location     behavior
```

Every layer takes your signal and sells it. You are the product. You get a "free" app in exchange for your nervous system.

Broccoli inverts it:

```
[You] ← broccoli ← encrypted store ← sensors + conversations
         ↑              ↑
    insight      automation
```

Same sensors. Same conversations. Same behavioral data. Pointed back at you.

- Your voice stress → "you were calmer today, here's why"
- Your touch hesitation → "this task frustrates you, want me to simplify it?"
- Your gaze → "you keep checking this app, here's the shortcut"
- Your conversations with every AI → searchable memory, personalizable model, zero retention by anyone else

No corporation profits. No government surveils. No ad network profiles. Just you, getting better at being you.

This is what "user-owned intelligence" means. Not a slogan. An architecture.
